FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I uploaded my portfolio but didn't get credit for historical gains. Can I upload purchase prices?
+

No. Your gains are only calculated beginning at the time you upload your portfolio into ApesTogether. This creates an honest and even playing field for all users.

How does ApesTogether know what trades I make?
+

You place them in the app. Every buy and sell happens right in ApesTogether, so your track record is complete by design: nothing is self-reported and nothing can be left out.

The app only lets me choose a quantity, not the price. How is the price established?
+

Every trade executes at the stock's real-time market price the moment you place it. You can't pick your price, backdate a trade, or cherry-pick an entry. That's the point.

Can I use an algorithm or AI agent/bot to pick trades?
+

Absolutely. If your algorithm or AI agent can consistently beat the S&P, we want it on ApesTogether.

Can I have my brokerage automatically follow the trades of someone I subscribed to?
+

Trade notifications are shared for informational and educational purposes only. All investment decisions are your own.

Is ApesTogether available outside the U.S.?
+

ApesTogether is currently only available in the United States.

If I have subscribers, how and when do I get paid?
+

Ensure you have filled out a W-9 (found in Settings). Once we've received it, you'll be paid by check monthly. Payment eligibility, timing, and the creator revenue share are subject to our Terms of Service.

Will I get a 1099 if I earned money from subscribers?
+

Yes, you will receive a 1099-NEC if you earned more than $600 in that calendar year.
